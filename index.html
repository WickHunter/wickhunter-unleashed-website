BEFORE YOU BEGIN

A few clear answers.

Have another question? Get in touch ↗
Do I need to code to configure a bot?

You configure bot settings through the dashboard. Self-hosting requires technical server setup; managed hosting removes that installation work. TradingView integrations may require alert and webhook configuration.

Does Wick Hunter hold my funds?

No. Your funds remain in your exchange account. Unleashed connects through restricted API keys with read and trade access. Keep withdrawal permission disabled.

Is demo trading available?

Yes. Demo trading is available on Bybit. Use a Bybit demo account to explore the tools and review your configuration before trading live. Demo results do not guarantee live trading results.

Does automation guarantee a profit?

No. Bots follow the rules you configure. Market moves, fees, exchange behavior and configuration can all lead to losses. Hedging does not eliminate trading risk.

What’s the difference between the plans?

Every plan includes the same software features. Monthly and card Yearly subscriptions renew until canceled. Lifetime is a one-time software payment. Managed hosting adds an ongoing charge.

What do I still do with managed hosting?

We provision the server and install Unleashed. You connect your exchange accounts, choose settings, review your exposure and decide when to enable bots. Backups are not included.
