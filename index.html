Non-custodial architecture

How it works with your money.

Three separate parties, three separate roles — software, installation, and exchange account never overlap.

01

Wick Hunter Software LLC

Develops, licenses, updates, and supports the software — it never touches your trading deposits.

02

Your private installation

Runs on your own server, with your exchange credentials and automation settings stored locally.

03

Your exchange account

Holds your assets and positions at a third-party venue, with withdrawal permission disabled for the software's API keys.
