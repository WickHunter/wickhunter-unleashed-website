Getting started

Plans & pricing

Where to find current plans, prices and hosting options.

Current plans, prices and hosting options are listed on the website: Plans & pricing. Final prices and payment terms are shown at checkout.

Good to know

  • Every plan includes the same bots, supported exchanges and software updates.
  • One server per licence. A second server using the same licence goes into exit-only mode.
  • Hosting is optional. Choose managed hosting, or run the app on your own VPS.
  • Renewals and licence extensions reach your app automatically. The new expiry shows under Settings → License; there is nothing to paste or reinstall.
  • Billing, invoices and your licence are in the customer dashboard.

If your licence expires

The app switches to exit-only mode: "closing and protection continue, nothing new opens." Your data stays on your server. Renew to resume new trades.

Managed-hosting servers are suspended after the hosting payment grace period. Export any settings you need before cancelling hosting.
