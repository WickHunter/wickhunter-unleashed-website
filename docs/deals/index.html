Using the app

Deals & Terminal

Read open deals, exchange status labels, and edit a deal in the Terminal.

Monitor and Manage Wick Hunter | Course 85:10 · Watch on YouTube ↗How a Wick Hunter Trade Works | Course 34:59 · Watch on YouTube ↗

Deals lists every open position on the selected account and market: which bot owns it, its average entry, filled DCA levels, take profit, stop and unrealized P&L. Press Details to open a deal's side panel.

Status labels

The app reads the exchange's full position list every 15 seconds. A deal is closed only after two complete reads agree it is gone; a failed read never closes anything.

LabelMeaning
Not on the exchange — confirmingThe last complete read no longer lists it. Waiting for a second read
Closed on the exchange — settlingClosed; the final figures are being matched
Not re-checked / Position read failingThe exchange could not be read. Check the exchange and your key
TP watched by the appNo resting take profit; the app closes at your target while it runs

Exchange position rows are positions no bot manages. They are read-only; viewing one does not take it over.

The total at the top adds only positions whose value is known. Anything still reconciling is left out and named beside the total.

Terminal

Terminal in the sidebar shows one deal on its own exchange's chart with every level marked.

  • Solid line: an order resting on the exchange. It works even if the app is down.
  • Dashed line: held by the app (for example a timed DCA). It only fires while the app runs.
  • Dotted line: planned, not placed yet.

Under the chart, a line counts orders by where they live, for example "3 resting on the exchange · 1 held by this app". Read it before maintenance.

Editing a deal

Drag the take profit or stop on the chart, or type a value. Nothing changes until you press Review & commit, which shows exactly how many orders will be cancelled and placed. Discard throws the change away. Edits apply to that one deal only; the bot's settings stay unchanged.

The order ticket sizes in $, the coin (for example SOL on SOLUSDT) or % balance. Check the unit before you send.
