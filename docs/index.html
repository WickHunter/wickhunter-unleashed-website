Welcome

Wick Hunter Unleashed

What Wick Hunter Unleashed is, the five bots, and where to start.

What Is Wick Hunter Unleashed? | Course 15:00 · Watch on YouTube ↗Meet Wick Hunter Unleashed0:37 · Watch on YouTube ↗

Wick Hunter Unleashed is private trading automation that runs on your own server. It places orders with your own exchange API key, and it never holds or withdraws your funds.

Warning. The honest warning: automated trading is risky. A bot does exactly what you configured, mistakes included. Leverage, slippage, fees, funding, exchange outages and fast markets can all lose money quickly. Trade only capital you can afford to lose. This guide is not financial advice and does not promise results.

Start here

  1. Quickstart: from your welcome email to a first small, protected trade.
  2. Connect your exchange: API key, permissions and Hedge Mode.
  3. Pick one bot from the table below and read its page.
  4. Risk management: set your limits before you scale up.
  5. Troubleshooting: what to do when something looks wrong.

The five bots

BotWhat starts a tradeBest for
Liq ScalperA large liquidation while price is stretched outside a band you setFading forced selling or buying on pairs you know
Optimized LiqThe same, with a band measured for each pair from its own historyWide pair lists
TV SignalYour TradingView alertStrategies you already run on TradingView
Manual BotYou. It manages trades you open yourselfAdding a DCA ladder, take profit and stop to your own entries
Hedge BotIt never opens the first trade. It hedges positions you already holdOffsetting a position that moves against you

Every bot except the Hedge Bot manages a deal the same way: an optional DCA ladder, a take profit and an optional stop loss. See Core concepts.

Before you trade

  • Know every order a bot can place, and the units of every size box.
  • Start small, with one bot, on one pair, and with a stop loss.
  • Know how to stop new trades (⏸ Pause on the bot) and how to close at the exchange.
  • Change one setting at a time and watch the result.

The app is the source of truth for labels and options. Videos may show an earlier version.
