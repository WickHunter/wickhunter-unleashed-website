Bots

Manual Bot

Let the app manage trades you open yourself with a DCA ladder, take profit and stop loss.

Manual Bot Overview | Wick Hunter Course 65:12 · Watch on YouTube ↗Manual Bot: Every Setting Explained | Wick Hunter9:12 · Watch on YouTube ↗

You open a trade yourself on the exchange. The Manual Bot notices it, takes it over ("picks it up") and manages it like any other deal: a DCA ladder, a take profit, and a stop loss if you switch one on. There is one Manual Bot per market.

  • It never opens a trade and never changes leverage.
  • It only picks up positions inside its direction and pair scope that no other bot manages.
  • With the Manual Bot off or paused, your hand-opened trades are left completely alone.
  • Pairs: All pairs or Only these. "All pairs" genuinely means all, including ones the exchange lists next month.
  • Direction and Pairs set the scope: they decide only what the bot takes on. Neither of these can abandon a position it already manages.
Warning. Turning it on is a commitment to every in-scope trade you open from then on, including a quick scalp you meant to close yourself. Narrow the direction and pairs first.

Recommended starter configuration

A suggestion, not a default. New forms start blank.

SettingStarter value
DirectionThe one side you plan to trade
PairsOnly these, with one pair
DCA orders0 (type 0)
Take profitFixed %, about 1%
Stop lossOn, Offset from Initial entry price

Picked up is not protected

Pickup normally takes about a second, and never more than about 15 seconds. You then get two messages:

  1. "picked up … it is placing the DCA ladder, take profit and stop loss now" (an attempt).
  2. "is now managing …" once the app has read back the orders the exchange accepted.

If no take profit lands within about half a minute, an UNPROTECTED alarm names the cause. Check the exchange.

The take profit and the stop loss rest on the exchange as reduce-only orders where the exchange accepts them. A position below the exchange minimum gets an app-watched exit instead, which only works while the app runs.

Pausing and deleting

  • ⏸ Pause stops new pickups. Deals it already manages keep their ladder, take profit and stop through to the exit.
  • Delete is refused while it still manages a position.
  • If positions are already open when you connect an account, a banner offers Turn on the Manual Bot & manage them or Leave them alone.

Why hasn't it picked up my trade?

Check in this order:

  1. The bot is not created, or is paused (My Bots).
  2. The trade is on a different account or market than the bot.
  3. Your direction or pair scope excludes it. Activity says "Left alone — …".
  4. Another bot already owns that pair, or has an unfinished order on it.
  5. Both sides of the pair are open and neither is tracked. Close one leg.
  6. It is not a perpetual position (spot balance, dated future).
