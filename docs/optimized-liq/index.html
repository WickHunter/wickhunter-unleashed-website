Optimized Liq

The Liq Scalper idea with a band measured for each pair from its own history.

Liq Scalper and Optimized Liquidation Bot | Course 45:22 · Watch on YouTube ↗Optimized Liquidation Bot: Every Setting Explained | Wick Hunter9:51 · Watch on YouTube ↗

The Optimized Liquidation Bot fades liquidations like the Liq Scalper, but you never type a band. For each pair it measures, from recent candles, how far price strays from its VWMA before coming back, and gives that pair its own long and short band. Everything after entry (DCA ladder, take profit, stop loss) works the same as the Liq Scalper.

Use it when your pair list is wide and mixes majors with small coins. "Optimized" means measured, not predicted or tuned for profit.

Recommended starter configuration

A suggestion, not a default. New forms start blank.

SettingStarter value
Pairs5–10 liquid pairs to begin with
Risk Level (long / short)2 / 2
VWMA Timeframe5 minutes
VWMA Period5
Lookback14 days (warms faster than the 30-day maximum)
Min Long / Min Short VWMA0 (no floor)
Deal settingsAs the Liq Scalper starter: small base order, 3–5×, DCA 0, TP ~1%, stop loss on

The five dials

  • Risk Level: how many candles in a row price may sit outside the band. Higher = tighter band = more entries and more trend risk.
  • Min Long / Min Short VWMA: a floor that can only widen a band.
  • VWMA Period: candles in the fair-value line.
  • VWMA Timeframe: candle size. Bigger = wider bands, fewer entries.
  • Lookback: days of history measured. Longer = wider bands.

Changing any dial throws away every pair's bands and recomputes them. The bot keeps managing open deals but opens nothing new until bands return. Change one dial at a time.

Warming up and stale

On day one the bot looks idle. That is expected.

  • Bands not computed yet (warming N/M candles): the pair is still loading history.
  • Stale — catching up: candles are older than three refresh periods (never less than 15 minutes); no entries on old data until it repairs.
  • No band: the pair never moved far enough in the lookback to measure.

Watch progress in Screener → Optimized Values.

Shared memory budget

Candle history is shared by every Optimized bot on this market: one pool per market. Adding pairs or lookback to one bot can silence one you did not touch, because pairs stop taking entries while the pool is over the candle memory budget. A save that would exceed it is refused, never trimmed. If a bot goes quiet after you changed another, look there first.

Common mistakes

  • A very wide pair list on a deep lookback: slow warm-up, and capacity refusals ("Live candle capacity limit reached"). Narrow the list.
  • Changing dials repeatedly, which restarts warm-up each time.
  • Raising Risk Level to get more trades: it tightens bands into trends.
