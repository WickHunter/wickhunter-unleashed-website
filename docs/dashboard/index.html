Using the app

Dashboard & My Bots

The header, the Dashboard, My Bots, sharing bots and the Screener.

Monitor and Manage Wick Hunter | Course 85:10 · Watch on YouTube ↗

The header decides what you see

The account and market selected in the header (for example Bybit · USDT perpetuals) decide what every page shows. A bot belongs to the account and market it was created on. If a page looks empty, check the header first.

Dashboard

Your daily overview: balance, today's P&L, open deals and recent events for the selected account.

  • The chart shows Equity or Wallet balance over 3h or Week. Hover or tap for exact values; CSV exports the series.
  • A dash means "not known yet", never zero.

My Bots

Every bot on the selected account and market, with its status and realized P&L.

  • Create Bot opens the bot gallery. Guided setup in the sidebar walks through the main screens.
  • ⏸ Pause stops new entries; open deals keep being managed. ▶ Resume starts it again.
  • Limits: up to 10 TV Signal bots, up to 10 Hedge Bots, up to 10 Liq Scalper and Optimized Liq bots combined, and one Manual Bot per market.

Share and import a bot

  • Share on a bot card gives its trading settings as Copy JSON, a download, or Copy link. Shares never contain API keys, webhook secrets or Discord addresses.
  • Import (next to Create Bot) → paste the link or JSON → Preview. Nothing is saved until you press the bot form's own Save. A shared Hedge Bot configuration is created paused with no pairs.

Screener

  • Liq stream: the live liquidation feeds, so you can see what a Liq bot is reacting to.
  • Optimized Values: per-pair bands and warm-up progress for Optimized Liq bots.

On your phone

Add the dashboard to your home screen (iPhone: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen; Android: Chrome's Install). It is the same page, talking to your own server.
