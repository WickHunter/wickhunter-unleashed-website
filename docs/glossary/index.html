Glossary

The terms used in the app and this guide.

TermMeaning
Account and marketAn exchange account plus a market (for example Bybit · USDT perpetuals), chosen in the header. Bots, deals and caps belong to one
Adopt / picked upThe Manual Bot taking over a position it didn't open. Picked up is not yet protected
AttachThe Hedge Bot watching a position. No order exists until a ladder distance is reached
Auto-attachWhether the Hedge Bot watches new positions automatically (Auto) or only those you attach (Manual only)
Base orderThe opening trade's position size (not margin)
Cross / isolated marginWhole account vs. only the position's margin backs a position
DCA ladderExtra orders that add to a losing position at set distances
DealOne position managed by one bot, with its own frozen settings
Exit-only modeLicence expired: exits and protection continue, nothing new opens
Hedge Mode / One-WaySeparate long and short positions per pair vs. one netted position. Hedge Mode is required
LiquidationA forced close by the exchange. Liq bots fade large liquidation prints
Market limitsCaps per account and market: Max deals, Max long, Max short, Margin used. Blank or 0 = no cap
Max w/o stopHow much hedge may be open without an armed stop
Paired take profitOne combined exit for the main position and its hedge
Reduce-onlyAn order that can only shrink a position
Resting vs app-watchedOn the exchange (works while the app is down) vs held by the app (works only while it runs)
Size % (Hedge)The total hedge to hold at a ladder level, as % of the main position
Stale / warmingData too old to trade / not enough history yet. New entries wait
Timed DCAA DCA held by the app on a clock rather than resting on the exchange
TrancheOne opposite-side hedge order on the ladder
Unresolved / reconcilingThe app is still matching a figure to exchange records. Never counted as zero
VWMA bandHow far price must be from its volume-weighted average before a Liq bot may enter
