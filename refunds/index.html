Refund Policy

Last updated: draft — not yet published

This policy covers cancellations and refunds for the Wick Hunter Unleashed monthly subscription licence.

1. Cancel anytime

You can cancel your subscription at any time from the billing portal. Cancelling stops future charges. Your licence remains active through the end of the period you already paid for — nothing is cut off early, and access does not extend past that date.

2. First-purchase refund window

If you are not satisfied with Unleashed after your first purchase, email us within 7 days of that purchase and we will consider a refund. Refund requests are reviewed individually by email at admin@wickhunterunleashed.com — please include your account email and the reason for the request. A Lifetime purchase is refundable within 7 days of purchase on request, on the same terms as a first payment.

3. Renewals

The 7-day window applies to your first purchase only. Subsequent monthly renewals are not eligible for refunds; cancel before a renewal date if you no longer want to be billed.

4. Chargebacks and disputes

Filing a chargeback or payment dispute instead of contacting us directly will result in immediate termination of your licence. We're glad to resolve billing issues by email — please reach out before disputing a charge with your bank or card issuer.

5. How refunds are issued

Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method through Stripe. Processing times depend on your bank or card issuer.

6. Contact

Billing and refund questions can be sent to admin@wickhunterunleashed.com.
